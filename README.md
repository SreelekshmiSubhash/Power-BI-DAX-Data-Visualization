# Power BI Assignment 2 – DAX & Data Visualization

## Overview

This repository contains my **Sales Analysis work on Power BI** as part of my **AI-Driven Data Analytics** learning journey.

The objective of this work was to build on my previous Power BI knowledge by working with **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** and creating different types of visualizations to analyze **E-Commerce Sales data**.

The assignment focused on creating calculated columns and measures, followed by developing visualizations to understand sales, profitability, targets, quantities, and regional performance.

## Dataset

The analysis uses three tables:

* **List of Orders**
* **Order Details**
* **Sales Target**

The dataset contains e-commerce sales information related to orders, products, categories, sub-categories, sales amounts, quantities, profits, locations, and sales targets.

---

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Calculated Columns

Created calculated columns in the **Order Details** table using DAX.

* Created **Category Type** by combining Category and Sub-Category.
* Created **Revenue per Order** using Amount and Quantity.
* Created **Sales Category** to classify orders as:

  * Above Average
  * Below Average

### 2. Calculated Measures

Created DAX measures to generate key analytical metrics.

* **Order Count** – calculated the total number of orders.
* **Average Profit in Delhi** – calculated the average profit for orders placed in Delhi.
* **Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales** – calculated accumulated sales from the earliest order date through each order date.

---

## Data Visualization

Created different Power BI visualizations to analyze sales performance and business trends.

### Sales Target Achievement by Category

Used a **Clustered Column Chart** to compare actual sales with sales targets across categories.

### Max Profit Margin by Sub-Category

Used a **Donut Chart** to analyze the maximum profit margin across different sub-categories.

### Monthly Sales Trend

Created a **Line Chart** to visualize monthly sales trends over time.

### Profit and Quantity by Sub-Category

Used a **Scatter Chart** to examine the relationship between profit and quantity sold across sub-categories.

### Total Sales Amount and Target

Created **Cards** to display total sales amount and sales target for quick comparison.

A **Multi-Row Card** was also created to display the minimum target for each segment.

### Sales Performance Matrix

Created a **Matrix** to compare actual sales against sales targets across different categories and months.

### Geographic Sales Analysis

Used a **Map** to visualize total sales by city and identify geographical sales patterns.

### Sales Distribution by Sub-Category

Created a **Treemap** to represent the distribution of sales across different sub-categories.

### Order Count Analysis by State

Created a **Funnel Chart** to visualize the distribution of order counts across different states.
